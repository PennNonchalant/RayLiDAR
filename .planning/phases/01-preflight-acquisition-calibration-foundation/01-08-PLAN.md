  <name>Task 1: Human overlay measurement and G1 sign-off</name>
  <files>artifacts/01/overlay_verification.json</files>
  <action>Present the overlay evidence and the measurement worksheet, collect the human's per-frame/camera feature counts and maximum offsets, and write only the reported values to `artifacts/01/overlay_verification.json`. Do not infer or invent measurements.</action>
  <what-built>
Calibration evidence and LiDAR→image overlays for three frames and both cameras:
- `artifacts/01/calib/calib_report.json` — transform, pose-chain, and camera-height checks
- `artifacts/01/overlays/frame_<frame>/image_00_overlay_4x.png` and `image_01_overlay_4x.png`
- `artifacts/01/overlays/overlay_report.json` — advisory edge-distance scores
- `artifacts/01/readiness.json` — dataset provenance, sizes, and hashes
- `artifacts/01/overlay_measurement_worksheet.md` — the five-feature table to fill
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
1. Open each `overlay_4x.png` (three frames × two cameras).
2. For at least five identifiable features per image (pole edge, building corner, road marking, sign, vehicle edge), measure the pixel offset between the LiDAR point colour and the feature edge. Ignore points on textureless surfaces where no edge exists.
3. Fill `artifacts/01/overlay_verification.json` with your measured values using this schema:
   ```json
   {
     "schema_version": 1,
     "reviewer": "<your name>",
     "measured_at_utc": "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ",
     "frames": [
       {"frame": 0, "camera": "image_00", "features_measured": 5, "max_offset_px": 1.0, "notes": ""}
     ]
   }
   ```
   Every sampled frame/camera needs an entry, `features_measured >= 5`, and `max_offset_px` is the **maximum** you measured.
4. Confirm `< 2 px` on every frame/camera, or report the failing frame and measured offset.
5. Do not record an offset you did not measure. If an image is too ambiguous to measure, say so; it is not a pass.
  </how-to-verify>
  <verify>

  </verify>
  <done>The human measurement file is schema-valid and every sampled frame/camera has at least five measured features.</done>
